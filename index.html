Meri Dil Ki Baatein

10 baatein… sab tumhare liye

Maafi

Maafi Aur Overreact Ka Ehsaas

Jaanu yaar, sorry kal ke liye. Mere andar jo khataq aata hai, mai bina clear kiye nahi reh pata hu. Mujhse nahi hota, koi bhi baat agar mujhe khataq rahi hai toh. Yaar pata nahi mai kaisa tha pehle aur ab kaisa hota ja raha hu, har baat par itna zyada overreact kyu hota hu yaar.

Darr

Khone Ka Darr Aur Shaq

Mujhe baatein khataqti hain tabhi aapko bolta hu, nahi toh mai ye sabhi baatein aapse nahi bolta. Mai nahi chahta aapko khona, isliye mai shaq karta hu. Mai ye nahi chahta ki mere saath jo hua woh wapas ho. Yaar mai nahi reh paunga aapke bina, isliye mujhe bohot darr lagta hai aur mai aapke upar shaq karta hu.

Shaq

Shaq Aur Screen Share Band

Agar mera shaq karna galat hai toh bata do, nahi karunga. Aaj ke baad se koi bhi shaq wagaira nahi karunga aur na hi phone ki screen share karne ke liye bolunga.

Gussa

Gusse Ki Baatein

Aur yaar ye kya baat hoti hai "kisi gair ko jism dikhati hu" wagaira wagaira bolne ki? Yaar mai gusse mein bol deta hu. Gair samajhte ho aap mujhe aise? Yaar jo baat mujhe pasand nahi hai, use justify karne se badhiya use khatam karne mein kya jaata hai aapka, aap ye batao?

Concern

Male Friends Ka Concern

Aapko zindagi mere saath bitani hai ya male friends ke saath, ye batao yaar. Aap nahi samajhte ho mai kyu aapko male friends ko hatane ke liye bolta hu. Jo acche hone ka dikhawa karte hain, woh waise hote nahi hain. Example mein mujhe hi dekh lo, pehle mai kaisa tha aur ab kaisa hu.

Kasam

Kasam Aur Snap Ki Baat

Aur rahi baat aapke jism ki, toh aap mujhe ek baar mana kardo ki aaj ke baad nahi dekhna hai, mai nahi dekhunga, baat khatam. Abhi tak aapne mujhe kasam de rakha hai ki mai koi snap nahi mang sakta hu. Jabse aapne kasam di hai tabse lekar aaj tak maine saamne se bola ki mujhe snap bana kar bhej do? Jab kasam nahi tha toh mai khud aapko saamne se bolta tha, par ab nahi bol sakta hu.

Request

Takleef Aur "Bhul Jao" Mat Bolo

Rahi bobos ki baat, toh usme bhi snap nahi bolta hu par aap samajh jaate ho ki zaroorat dekhna hai mujhe. Aur haan, chahe mai kitni bhi ladai kyu na karlu, par aap please mat bola karo mujhe "bhul jao", bohot takleef hota hai ise sunkar.
